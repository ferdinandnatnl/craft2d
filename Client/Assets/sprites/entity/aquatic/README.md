# Aquatic entity sprites

The `*_live.png` sprites come from side projections of Mojang's vanilla fish
models and entity texture atlases. Tail layers are separate so they can animate
while the fish swim. The raw fish inventory sprites remain the loot drops.

Source: [Mojang Bedrock Samples](https://github.com/Mojang/bedrock-samples),
under `resource_pack/models/entity` and `resource_pack/textures/entity/fish`.
